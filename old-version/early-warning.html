Early Warning Risk

Dito lumalabas ang at-risk learners based on rules like low attendance, falling grades, at behavior flags:

  • Risk Detection: Automatic identification ng learners na may risk factors
  • Rule-Based Triggers: Low attendance, falling grades, at behavior flags
  • Intervention Prioritization: You use it to prioritize interventions based sa risk level
  • Counseling Scheduling: Schedule at track counseling sessions efficiently
  • Next-Best Actions: Recommended actions para sa each at-risk learner

Goal nito ay ma-detect early ang problems bago lumala para timely intervention.
